# Roadmap

0. Foundation: models, validation, risk baseline, audit logger and tests.
1. Licensed/provider-compliant data adapters and market calendars.
2. Versioned strategy specifications and indicator library.
3. Event-driven backtester with market-specific costs and execution assumptions.
4. Walk-forward, holdout, robustness and multiple-testing controls.
5. Paper execution simulator and portfolio accounting.
6. Dashboard, monitoring, persistence and cloud deployment.
7. Independent security, statistical and regulatory audit.

Live trading is outside the current scope and requires separate explicit approval.
